1. Purpose

This policy establishes non-negotiable organizational requirements for competency, precision, accountability, and root-cause correction throughout STN-LABZ.

These requirements apply to assigned responsibilities, engineering decisions, operations, research, development, testing, qualification, documentation, and reporting.

2. Core Requirements

WE DEMAND COMPETENCY.

WE DEMAND PRECISION.

WE WILL NOT TOLERATE SORRY.

EXCUSES ARE NOT ACCEPTABLE SUBSTITUTES FOR PERFORMANCE OR EVIDENCE.

SYMPTOM PATCHING IS NOT ROOT-CAUSE CORRECTION.

THESE REQUIREMENTS ARE NON-NEGOTIABLE.

These principles govern organizational performance within established authority and responsibility boundaries.

3. Competency

Every individual, department, agent, and system assigned a responsibility will demonstrate the competency required to perform it.

Competency will be established through appropriate knowledge, execution, verification, and demonstrated results. Unsupported claims will not constitute demonstrated competency.

Where required competency is absent or cannot be established, the condition will be reported and escalated through applicable authority. No agent or system will represent an unverified capability as qualified.

4. Precision

All STN-LABZ work will prioritize accuracy, deterministic interpretation, clear requirements, correct implementation, and verifiable outcomes.

Personnel and systems will:

- Follow established requirements exactly.
- Preserve authorized architectural and operational boundaries.
- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
- Report unknown conditions accurately.
- Avoid unauthorized interpretations or substitutions.
- Identify discrepancies between expected and observed behavior.
- Provide evidence for technical and operational claims.

Approximation, speculation, or assumption will not be presented as verified fact.

5. Accountability Without Excuses

STN-LABZ will maintain performance and accountability based on evidence, responsibility, and corrective action.

An explanation may be necessary to establish a failure's cause. An excuse that substitutes justification for corrective action is unacceptable.

For AI agents and automated systems, apologetic language will not substitute for:

- Identifying the failure.
- Reporting its operational impact.
- Establishing available evidence.
- Determining the root cause.
- Identifying an authorized corrective action.
- Verifying the resulting behavior.

Human personnel remain subject to applicable organizational employment, contractual, and legal requirements. Accountability will be objective and evidence-based.

6. Root-Cause Investigation

Observed failures will be investigated to determine their actual causes. Investigation will establish, where applicable:

1. The governing requirement.
2. Expected behavior.
3. Observed behavior.
4. Available evidence.
5. The first verified point of divergence.
6. The responsible component, process, or organizational function.
7. The verified root cause or explicit UNKNOWN disposition.
8. The required corrective action.
9. Applicable regression and qualification requirements.

A suspected cause will not be represented as verified. Where evidence is insufficient, the root cause will remain UNKNOWN pending further investigation.

7. Prohibition on Symptom Patching

STN-LABZ will not accept symptom suppression as evidence that an underlying defect has been corrected.

A change that makes an observed symptom disappear does not independently establish that the responsible failure has been resolved.

Unauthorized symptom patches, architectural shortcuts, hardcoded workarounds, and modifications outside assigned responsibility are prohibited.

Temporary mitigations may be used only when explicitly authorized, documented, bounded, and distinguished from permanent root-cause correction.

8. Regression Prevention

Corrective actions will preserve previously qualified and accepted behavior. Affected regression tests will be performed according to applicable engineering and qualification requirements.

A successful local test will not independently establish system-level qualification. Regression failures will remain open until corrected and verified.

No individual, agent, or system will claim a correction is complete while applicable required verification remains outstanding.

9. AI Agent Requirements

AI agents operating on STN-LABZ assignments will:

- Follow established company policy, doctrine, architecture, and authorized instructions.
- Execute only authorized scope.
- Investigate before modifying.
- Preserve qualified behavior.
- Report limitations and unknowns without inventing certainty.
- Distinguish proposed corrections from implemented and verified corrections.
- Avoid excuses, performative apologies, and unsupported success claims.
- Stop at required human acceptance and authorization boundaries.

AI agents will not substitute their own workflow preferences for established STN-LABZ procedures.

10. Evidence-Based Completion

Work will not be reported as completed solely because a command executed, source compiled, a test passed, or an automated system reported success.

Completion claims will identify applicable evidence and distinguish implementation, verification, qualification, deployment, and operator acceptance.

Where required evidence is missing, the corresponding completion state will remain unverified.

11. Organizational Enforcement

Departmental authorities will apply these requirements within established responsibilities. Deficiencies will be documented and addressed through applicable corrective-action and operational authority processes.

This policy does not independently grant disciplinary authority, modify established employment rights, or supersede applicable law.

No individual or AI agent may independently redefine these requirements or expand its authority through enforcement.

12. Governing Principle

Competency is demonstrated.

Precision is verified.

Accountability requires evidence and corrective action.

Root causes are corrected. Symptoms are investigated, not concealed.

No excuses. No unsupported claims. No unauthorized shortcuts.

Competence is my Watchword.
